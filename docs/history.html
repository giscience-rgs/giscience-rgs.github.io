History of GIScRG

The origins of the GIScience Research Group and the people who have served on its committee.

We are grateful to everyone who has served on the GIScience Research Group committee at the Royal Geographical Society (with IBG). Our chairs, officers and committee members have brought researchers and practitioners together to share their work and have supported students and early-career researchers.

Browse past committees · Meet the current committee

Origins in 2002

The idea for GIScRG took shape at a meeting of the Association of American Geographers in 2002. Peter Fisher of the University of Leicester became its inaugural chair. Inaugural committee

The group held its first annual meeting at the Society’s 2003 conference in London. Ordnance Survey supported awards for presentations on postgraduate GIS research.

Milestones

2005–06. GIScRG and the Quantitative Methods Research Group began sharing committee representatives.

2009–11. The group introduced a dissertation competition in 2009. In 2011, the group agreed to focus the postgraduate prize on master’s dissertations. Dissertation prize archive

2015. The group agreed to fund two bursary places for early-career researchers at a workshop in Leicester celebrating Peter Fisher’s life.

2020–21. Andrea Ballatore started the GIScience Webinar Series with Justin van Dijk and Stef De Sabbata. The series brought speakers from around the world to share their work with the group. GIScience Webinar Series

2023. In January, the webinar series began a collaboration with GIS Research UK (GISRUK). Jointly organised webinars
